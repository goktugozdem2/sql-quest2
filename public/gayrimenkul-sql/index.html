5 tablo, real gayrimenkul schema

NYC OpenData (opendata.cityofnewyork.us). NYC OpenData Terms — commercial redistribution permitted with attribution. Manhattan + Brooklyn fokuslu, 4400+ row, gerçek BBL ve owner isimleri.

properties
500 rows
PLUTO — bbl, borough, address, bldg_class, year_built, assess_total, owner_name
sales
500 rows
ACRIS Master — deed/mortgage documents, document_amt, document_date
sales_legals
1500 rows
Document → BBL bridge — bir deed birden çok lot kapsayabilir
sales_parties
1354 rows
Buyer + seller per document — LLC analizleri için ideal
permits
500 rows
DOB permit issuance — construction activity, owner business types
