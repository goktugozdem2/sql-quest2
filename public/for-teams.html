SQL Quest for cohorts

Timed SQL practice for your whole cohort.

Seats for bootcamps, master's programs and SQL instructors. Every learner gets SQL Quest Pro. You get each learner's Skillmap and progress, so you can see who is ready for a technical screen and who is not, one skill at a time.

What a seat is

One Pro account per learner, for the length of your cohort.

  • 300+ runnable SQL challenges

    Easy through Hard, run in the browser against real tables. Nothing to install, nothing for your team to host.

  • Timed mock interviews

    Company-style screens on a clock and scored, including the 60-minute Revolut and 70-minute Capital One formats.

  • A tutor that reads the wrong query

    It looks at what the learner actually wrote and names the defect, one step at a time. Pro lifts the free tier's daily cap.

  • A Skillmap for every learner

    Nine skills, scored from what each learner solves and misses, not from a self-assessment.

Questions

Do learners need to install anything?

No. Everything runs in the browser, including the database each challenge queries.

Can a learner keep practising after the cohort ends?

Yes. The seat ends, the account does not: every solve, the Skillmap and the Coach stay with the learner; the Pro challenges and the timed mocks lock again.

Who am I talking to?

The founder, Göktuğ, who builds SQL Quest. Seats are set up by hand while there are only a few cohorts, which is also why the instructor report is written by a person and not a dashboard.
