The Two SQL Rounds

What the Revolut data analyst SQL rounds ask

As described publicly in September 2026 — one prep guide synthesised from 27 candidate reports stamped Q3 2026, not Revolut itself. Where the reports disagree, this page says so rather than picking a number.

⏱️

Round one: the HackerRank screen

About 60 minutes, online: two written SQL questions plus multiple-choice items over user and transaction tables. One report puts it at "two SQL questions and several" multiple choice; an earlier edition of the same guide said 30 minutes, the Q3 2026 reports say 60.

🧑‍💻

Round two: the live session

Four sequential questions on interconnected users, transactions, events and subscriptions tables: monthly active users with completed transactions, the top 10% of users by transaction volume, joins, CTEs, date logic, window functions, cohort and conversion analysis.

🎯

What costs points

Counting transactions where users were asked for, a forgotten status filter (completed, not attempted), WHERE where HAVING belongs, and NULL treated as a value — the candidates name COALESCE by name. The grain first, then the filter, then the number.

Sources: interviewquery, "Revolut Data Analyst Interview Guide 2026", synthesised from 27 candidate reports stamped Q3 2026 (read September 2026). An earlier edition of the same guide gave 30 minutes for the screen; the 2026 reports say 60. Revolut does not publish the format of its rounds; treat every specific above as candidate-reported, and expect it to change.

Practice the screen under the clock: a 60-minute HackerRank-style SQL mock on a synthetic multi-currency neobank ledger — six multiple-choice questions read off real query output, then the two written tasks the reports name first: monthly active users with completed transactions, and the top 10% of users by volume.
