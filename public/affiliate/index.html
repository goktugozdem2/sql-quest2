FAQ

Stuff you'd want to know.

How much do I earn per referral?+

30% recurring for the lifetime of every Pro subscription. Pro plans are $19/month, $99/year, or $199 lifetime. Monthly subscribers compound — you keep earning 30% as long as they stay subscribed. Lifetime sales pay $59.70 once but are easier to convert.

When and how do I get paid?+

Monthly payouts via Wise (international) or bank transfer (US/Turkey). Minimum payout: $25. Net-30 terms — earnings from January are paid in February. We send a statement before each payout so you can audit. TRY/IBAN supported for Turkish partners.

How does attribution work?+

Last-click attribution with a 30-day cookie window. Every partner gets a unique link like sqlquest.app/?ref=yourname. We track click → signup → Pro conversion server-side, so even if the user clears cookies between signup and purchase, attribution holds (we stamp your code onto their account at signup).

Do I need to commit to a post or volume?+

No. Recommend SQL Quest when it fits naturally for your audience — a sponsored post, a tweet, a newsletter mention, a Reddit comment, or just a link in your bio. No minimum activity. Inactive partners stay attributed; active ones earn more.

Can I promote LeetCode, DataCamp, DataLemur too?+

Yes. No exclusivity. We trust your audience knows what fits them best. If your readers want SQL Quest for some uses and DataLemur for others, recommend both honestly. Not every product fits every audience — we'd rather you stay credible.

What if my audience is small (under 5,000)?+

Apply anyway. We're prioritizing engaged audiences over raw size. A 1k-subscriber newsletter where readers actually open and click can outperform a 100k channel with passive followers. Niche fit matters more than volume.

How do I apply?+

Email can@sqlquest.app with your channel link, audience size, and one sentence on why your audience would care about SQL Quest. We respond within 48 hours, usually same-day. If approved, you get your custom link and a free lifetime Pro account within 24 hours.

Honest answer: most outreach gets ignored, and conversion varies wildly. A typical newsletter mention converts 0.5-3% of clickers to Pro. Some partners earn $50/month, some earn $5,000. We're transparent about this — we'd rather you set realistic expectations than feel oversold.
