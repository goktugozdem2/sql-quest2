Where SQL Quest Wins

5 things StrataScratch grinders ask for

Common gripes from candidates who hit the StrataScratch paywall or got tired of staring at "wrong" with no diagnosis.

1

A free tier that's actually free

StrataScratch's free plan is 75+ questions — a fair first pass, but the other 900+ and every AI feature are Premium. SQL Quest's free tier is the product — 195 of 257 challenges (every Easy and Medium plus 6 Hard previews), the AI Coach (daily quota), the 9-skill radar, every sector track, all 22 company pages. Most candidates ship interviews on the free tier alone.

2

Wrong-answer diagnostic

Your query produces wrong results. StrataScratch shows you the expected table — squint and find the diff yourself. SQL Quest tells you: "Row 3, column total_revenue, your value 4,820 — expected 4,920. You included refunded orders." Same wrong query, two completely different debugging experiences.

3

AI Coach (Claude) in the editor

Stuck on a window function? Type the question in plain English. Claude reads your query, schema, and expected output and explains what's actually wrong — on the free tier, with a daily quota. StrataScratch's AI chat assistant is Premium-only and metered by credits; on the free plan you have the discussion boards.

4

Adaptive Coach (not a flat list)

StrataScratch is a question library — you decide what to do next. SQL Quest's Coach picks the next step: lesson when you're missing a concept, drill when a skill is rusty, mastery check when you're ready to test up. Less decision fatigue, more forward motion.

5

Quantified weakness radar

SQL Quest's 9-axis radar shows which skills are weak in numbers (Subqueries & CTEs at 32, Window Functions at 41, Joins at 78). The Coach then drills the weak axes specifically. StrataScratch tags topics on questions but doesn't aggregate them into a measurement you can act on.

When StrataScratch is the better pick

Not a hatchet job. Real cases where StrataScratch wins.

📚 Pick StrataScratch if:

You're prepping for a data-scientist or ML role that tests SQL plus Python or R, or hands you a take-home. StrataScratch covers all three languages and lists 50+ data projects with cloud notebooks; SQL Quest is SQL-only by design.

You want AI mock interviews. StrataScratch Premium includes an AI interviewer with grading; SQL Quest's Mock Interview mode is a timed question set, not an interviewer.

You've already exhausted SQL Quest's 257 challenges and want pure volume. StrataScratch lists 1000+ coding questions across its three languages.

Verified provenance matters to you — "this is the actual question Meta asked in 2024" with high confidence on the source. StrataScratch curates with strong sourcing claims.

You learn well from peer discussion threads. StrataScratch's community is active; SQL Quest leans on AI explanation instead.

Your interview is in 1-2 weeks and you want maximum cram-bank density. SQL Quest's adaptive Coach is built for the 4-12 week ramp; StrataScratch's flat list is faster to grind through.

If two of those bullets describe you, StrataScratch is probably the right call. If three of them don't, SQL Quest is faster and cheaper.
