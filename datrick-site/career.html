Open roles

Senior Data Engineer

Remote · Full-time or contract

What you'll do

  • Own client engagements end to end: scoping, delivery, handover
  • Build and rescue ETL/ELT pipelines and warehouse models
  • Run migration QA with parallel-run reconciliation
  • Stand up BI reporting and KPI systems stakeholders trust
  • Ship LLM-backed workflows with evaluation gates and human review
  • Write the runbooks the next engineer will thank you for

What we look for

  • 5+ years operating production data environments
  • Strong SQL and data modeling fundamentals
  • Experience with modern warehouse and transformation stacks
  • Comfort working directly with client stakeholders
  • Written communication that stands on its own
  • Judgment about when automation needs a human in the loop

To apply, email info@datrick.com with a short note about a data environment you made boring (in the good way), plus your CV or LinkedIn.
