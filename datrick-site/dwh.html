Data Warehouse Services

A data warehouse is the central collection of data your decision-makers examine to make better decisions. We design, build, migrate, and operate warehouses worth trusting.

One source of truth, continuously fed

Transactional systems, relational databases, SaaS platforms, and event streams all feed a warehouse regularly and continuously via ETL. When that flow is designed well, reporting is boring — in the best sense.

Datrick builds warehouses around your reporting requirements and your team's ability to maintain them, then leaves behind the documentation and runbooks to keep it that way.

  • Warehouse architecture and platform selection
  • Schema design: staging, core models, marts
  • Continuous ETL/ELT feeds from all sources
  • Access control, governance, and cost management
  • Ongoing operation under DBA/NOC coverage
Migrations

Warehouse migrations without the cliff edge

Platform moves and consolidations are where environments break. Our migration engagements are structured to remove the cliff edge.

Inventory & lineage

Catalog every source, model, report, and consumer before anything moves. Unknown dependencies are how cutovers fail.

Custom migration plan

A staged plan with minimal downtime, agreed cutover conditions, and a rollback path — written down before work begins.

Parallel run & reconciliation

Old and new run side by side while automated reconciliation checks — including LLM-assisted migration QA with human review — prove the numbers match.

Cutover & handover

Cutover happens only when the evaluation criteria pass. Your team receives documentation, not a dependency on us.

Operating model
