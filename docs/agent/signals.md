next: a verdict whose **Next** is not "keep", an open finding of a dated read,
or a pattern in user feedback. Triage picks bets from here; when a bet is
taken, add its PR number to the row's bet cell, and do not delete the row.

  one is a hunch and does not belong here.
- **Size** is the cost of the suggested bet: S ≤ 1 day, M 2–3 days, L a week
  or more.
- Source is one of: verdict · feedback · research · data read.
