# B4 — Money

**Lane:** the monetization plan.
**Cadence:** monthly (the 1st), or when the founder asks.
**Judged by:** ideas from its plan that shipped and hit.

1. Re-read the money funnel (`docs/plans/monetization-ideas-2026-11-22.md` §1, same queries) and the ledger verdicts on M-01…M-12.
2. Re-fetch competitor prices and write a new dated read in `docs/reads/`. Never quote a price without one.
3. Write `docs/plans/monetization-ideas-<date>.md`:
   - what moved;
   - what to stop;
   - up to 5 new ranked ideas, each with data, a metric, a falsification and a test plan, tagged AGENT-NOW or FOUNDER.

   AGENT-NOW ideas go to B1's queue as BACKLOG lines.
4. One docs PR, self-merged.
