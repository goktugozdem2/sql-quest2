**Company name:** SQL Quest

**Describe what your company does in 50 characters or less.**
AI tutor for SQL interviews that won't cheat you
(48 characters; alternative: "Personalized SQL interview practice with an AI tutor")

**What is your company going to make?**
People preparing for data analyst and data engineer interviews practise SQL
on question banks that only say "wrong". SQL Quest runs every query in the
browser, diagnoses what the result got wrong (missing rows, wrong values,
wrong grain), maps the person's nine SQL skills, and picks the next question
from the weakest one. The AI tutor, built on Claude, coaches to the fix in
steps instead of pasting the answer. For a specific employer, the person gets
a company set and a timed mock modelled on what candidates report about that
screen.

**What's new about what you're making? What do people do now?**
They grind LeetCode-style banks, which grade pass/fail, or ask a general
chatbot, which hands over a full answer and teaches nothing for the day of
the interview. We sit between the two: graded practice on real data with a
tutor that holds the answer back until the person has tried.

**Who are your competitors? What do you understand that they don't?**
DataLemur, StrataScratch, LeetCode's SQL set, and general AI chat. What we
understand: the buyer is not "learning SQL", the buyer has an interview on a
date. Our payers were preparing for a specific employer's screen, and none of
that showed up in analytics; it came from talking to them. So the product is
organised around the interview: the date, the company, the weak skill.
