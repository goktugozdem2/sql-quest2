# Partners pipeline (P1)

One row per organisation. Kept by P1 (`docs/agent/fleet/P1-partnerships.md`).
**Organisations and organisational routes only.** Routes are a partnership
or business page, a contact form, or a generic inbox published on the
organisation's own site. No personal name or personal address goes in this
file; anything person-shaped, and the Gmail draft and thread IDs, live
outside the repo in `~/.claude/sqlquest-partners.json`.

Stages: `found → qualified → draft ready → sent (founder) → replied → call →
pilot → paid / partner live → lost (with why)`.

Priority: **A** = SQL is central, cohorts run now or classes are large, a
B2B or programme route exists, and the learners look like our buyers
(interview-bound analysts, fintech, career switchers). **B** = good fit, weak
on one of those. **C** = weak fit, competitor, or pivoted.

**What we offer today: a free cohort pilot** (Pro for one cohort, size
stated, seats granted by hand, a short weekly note to the teacher). The seat
price the founder picked on 2026-09-26 (Option B: $39 a seat for a 3-month
cohort pass, 10-seat minimum) may be quoted once an organisation asks about
paying (`orchestrator-decisions.md`, 2026-11-22); first notes offer the free
pilot only.

Sources: the 40 rows from 2026-09-26 come from
`docs/outreach/teams-2026-09-26.md` (read there for the September notes).
Rows marked *re-read 11-22* were opened again on their own site on
2026-11-22. Rows marked *new 11-22* were found and qualified that day.
