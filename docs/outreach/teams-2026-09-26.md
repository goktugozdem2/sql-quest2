# Team seats: targets, offer, first five emails (2026-09-26)

**Why this exists.** O1 is 50 paying customers a month by 2026-12-08, from 2.
Individual sales can't close that gap alone (`docs/agent/objectives.md`: it
needs 6× traffic *and* 4× conversion). On 2026-09-26 the founder decided that
**team seats count toward the 50 and are reported separately**. This file is
the seat sale, ready for outreach this week:

1. who to write to (40 organisations, with public organisational routes only),
2. the offer, as two price options for the founder to pick,
3. five drafts to A-priority organisations. **Nothing has been sent.**

The door that goes with it is `/for-teams/` (`src/for-teams.html`). It is
noindex, not in the sitemap and linked from nowhere until the founder
publishes it. It records `team_interest {seats, email?}`. Row 6 of
`docs/plans/monetization-2026-09-24.md` is the hypothesis and the kill rule.

**How the list was built.** Three read-only research passes on 2026-09-26
opened each organisation's own site. "Fetched" means the page was read in
full. "Snippet" means only a search result was seen, so check it before
relying on it. **Contact routes are organisational only**: partnership or
business pages, contact forms, and generic inboxes published on the
organisation's own site. Where a page showed only a named person's address,
the page is recorded and the person is not. No personal emails are collected
here, and nothing about private individuals.

Priority: **A** = SQL is central, cohorts run often or are large, and there
is a B2B or program route. **B** = good fit but weaker on one of those.
**C** = weak fit, competitor, or pivoted.

## 2. The seat offer: two price options, the founder picks one

**What a seat is, in both options.** This is the same thing `/for-teams/`
says, and nothing more. A seat is one SQL Quest Pro account per learner for
the length of the cohort. It includes Hard challenges, the timed mock bank
(including the Revolut 60-minute and Capital One 70-minute formats), the
tutor past the free daily cap, and the Skillmap.

**There is no instructor dashboard.** For the first cohorts the founder
grants each seat by hand. He also sends the instructor a weekly summary per
learner (nine Skillmap scores, solves, mock results), read from
`users.data`. Learners are told at sign-up that their instructor receives
it. When a seat ends, the account drops to the free tier and nothing is
taken away.

**Market anchors** (public pricing pages, read 2026-09-26):
- Maven Analytics for Business: $34 a seat a month, minimum 2.
- 365 Data Science business plan: 2-seat minimum, discounts from 30 seats.
- Dataquest teams: $283 a licence a year.
- Our own list prices: $29 a month or $99 a year.

A seat has to sit clearly below $29 a month to be worth an instructor's
paperwork. It also has to sit above the annual plan's $8.25 a month, or
the buyer is better off telling each learner to buy annual.

### Option A: per seat, per month, 10-seat minimum

- **$15 a seat a month, minimum 10 seats** ($150 a month floor).
- Billed monthly by Stripe invoice to the organisation. Seats can be added or
  dropped at the next invoice, and the organisation can stop in any month.
- Fits bootcamps with rolling cohorts, course businesses that bundle
  practice into a paid tier, and career offices trying one term.
- At 35 seats: **$525 a month**, and it recurs only while cohorts run.

### Option B: cohort pass, 3 months a seat, paid once

- **$39 a seat for 3 months, minimum 10 seats** ($390 floor, about $13 a seat
  a month). It does not renew; a new cohort buys a new pass.
- One invoice, paid up front. That matches how programs budget: per cohort,
  per term, on a purchase order.
- Fits university programs (term-shaped and procurement-shaped) and
  bootcamps with fixed 12-week cohorts.
- At 35 seats: **$1,365 once**, covering October to December.

**Recommendation (the founder may overrule it): lead with B.**
- For the buyer, it is one decision and one invoice.
- It needs no monthly seat-count conversation, which matters while there is
  no dashboard.
- A 3-month pass bought in October still counts in the December read.

Keep A as the answer to "we run rolling monthly cohorts."

### The arithmetic to ~35 seats by early December

Seats sold by about **2026-11-15** are live for the **2026-12-08** read.
Working back from that date:
- first emails 09-29;
- replies in the first half of October;
- a pilot decision by late October;
- seats live early to mid November.

- **About 35 seats is roughly 3 deals of 10–15 seats.** Alternatively, it is

  one bootcamp.
- The conservative column misses by about 23 seats. The cheap hedge is a
  **second wave of about 20 organisations around 10-13**, written with what
  the first wave's replies taught. It is not a follow-up to the same people:
  no sequence, as the email rule says.
- University procurement is the slowest route (vendor forms, purchase
  orders, net-30). Expect program deals to close after early December unless
  a career office can pay from a discretionary budget. Bootcamps and course
  businesses are the realistic December seats.

**Reporting.** Team seats count toward the 50 and are reported separately.
- Grant each seat from the SQL path with
  `proGrantReason = 'team_seat:<org-slug>'`. A client write is ignored, so
  this is the only path that works.
- That makes "active team seats" one count over `users.data`, and it never
  mixes with `stripe_webhook` purchases.
- Seat money arrives by Stripe invoice, not through the Payment Links, so it
  never passes through `pro_purchase_completed`. Log each deal by hand (org,
  seats, option, invoice id, start and end) until volume says otherwise.

**Kill rule for the door.** It was written before the data, in row 6 of the
monetization plan. If `/for-teams/` gets fewer than 3 `team_interest` rows
(distinct `aid`) in the 30 days after it is published, drop the page and
write it down. The outreach is judged separately, by replies and deals.

Read side for the door:

```sql
select created_at::date as day,
       ((metadata #>> '{}')::jsonb)->>'aid'           as aid,
       (((metadata #>> '{}')::jsonb)->>'seats')::int  as seats,
       ((metadata #>> '{}')::jsonb) ? 'email'         as left_email
from pro_events
where event = 'team_interest' and reason = 'landing'
order by created_at desc;
```

- The email itself is in the same metadata, under `email`.
- `pro_events` is insert-only for anon, so a browser cannot read a row back.
- `pro-events-setup.sql` describes a 90-day cleanup. If that job is
  scheduled, copy any email out within 90 days.
