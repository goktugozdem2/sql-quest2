## Problem Statement

The founder asked for a module that analyses a person's hiring readiness against
their goal, and for features that raise the probability of getting hired.

The session found that most of it already exists and almost nobody reaches it.

## Demand Evidence

Intent is captured from people who answer the question. Of the 317 who have,
**228 (72%) declared `interview` or `job_ready`.** Their behaviour differs:

| Intent | People | Reached 6 solves | Shown the Pro modal | Clicked checkout | Shown → click |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| interview | 118 | 52 | 53 | 4 | **7.5%** |
| job_ready | 110 | 58 | 57 | 2 | 3.5% |
| learning | 89 | 44 | 46 | **0** | **0.0%** |

The positive signal is weak — 4 clicks is not a result, and
`sqlquest-n4-payers-cannot-carry-a-premise` warns against exactly this. **The
negative signal is strong:** learning-intent users have been shown the modal 46
times and have never once clicked. Money is on the interview side of the split.

Corroborating: both payers we can name were preparing for the Capital One
CodeSignal screen (`capital-one-payer-signal`).

**What does not exist: any outcome data at all.** Confirmed with the founder
this session — nobody has ever told this product whether they got an interview,
a rejection, or a job.

## Status Quo

Not "users have no solution." Users have our solution and cannot open it.

- Eight mock interviews ship in `src/data/mock-interviews.js`. Seven are generic
  ("Tech Startup", "SaaS Company", "Big Tech"). **One names a real company and a
  real assessment format:** `capital-one-codesignal`, 14 questions.
- The interview countdown card is built, tested (93 tests,
  `src/utils/interview-prep.js`), merged, EN + TR, and **dark** behind
  `FEATURE_FLAGS.features.interviewCountdown = false`.
- `showLegacyPrimaryNav` is hard-coded `false` (`src/app.jsx:7720`), so
  `activeTab === 'trials'` is reachable only through the `?interview=<id>` deep
  link on company pages and the onboarding `goal === 'interview'` branch.
- **Lifetime: 22 accounts carry any interview history, against 1,179 people who
  have viewed the Coach.** In `pro_events`, **7 people** have ever produced an
  interview or mock event, and none of those rows carry a mock id — so we cannot
  tell which mock anyone opened.

The codebase already named this, on 2026-09-08: *"The card was built onto a door
with no handle."*

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

**The user:** someone with a dated SQL screen at a named company. Not "job
seekers." The two payers we can name were three weeks out from one specific
assessment.

**The wedge (founder's call, D2):** a timed mock of their real screen — the
format, the clock, the score, the wrong-answer breakdown. Chosen over the Hard
bank, the daily plan, and a shareable credential because it is the only one that
rehearses the event the buyer is actually afraid of, and because the Capital One
version already exists.

**But the wedge is not what ships next.** See Recommended Approach.

## Constraints

- **Nothing monetisation-adjacent ships before 2026-09-29** (`objectives.md`).
  This blocks pricing the mock.
- **The `interviewCountdown` flip is gated behind the 2026-09-20 paywall-surfaces
  read.** One surface, one change at a time.
- **The 2026-09-13 activation read** (105-as-opener) closes in two days and
  covers first-contact routing.
- **No prediction, ever.** `tests/interview-prep.test.js` fails the build on
  prediction words in both languages, mutation-verified. This stays.

## Premises

All five agreed by the founder (D4):

1. We cannot ship a hiring-probability number. No outcome has ever come back.
2. A job-seeker pays for rehearsal of the specific event, not guidance toward it.
3. Screens get harder as assistants write the SQL — judging a query you did not
   write is the scarce skill, and a timed screen tests it better, not less.
4. The module mostly exists. This is sequencing and pricing, not a build.
5. Outcome data is the missing instrument and it is cheap.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Put a handle on the door — CHOSEN
Route the 228 people who declared `interview` / `job_ready` into the interview
surface instead of the generic practice list. `applyIntentRouting` is already
written behind `intentRouting: false`. Effort S (human ~2 days / CC ~2 hours).
Risk Low. Reuses: the flag, the routing function, the whole existing surface.

**Built 2026-09-12, dark behind the same flag, flips 2026-09-13.** The
description above overstated what `applyIntentRouting` did: it opened the
whole bank and applied the arrival company; it never touched the Interview
tab. What ships is the actual handle — a third primary-nav tab, shown only
after the first solve and only to people with hiring intent, interview
history, the interview-prep goal, or a company-page arrival
(`src/utils/interview-nav.js`, tested). The intent answer does **not**
yank anyone into the tab: it appears, and they choose. Plus the three events
the read needs (`interview_tab_viewed`, `interview_started`,
`interview_completed`, all carrying the mock id — the prerequisite below is
met by this change). Metric: `interview_reach` in `docs/agent/metrics.md`.

### Approach B: Price the one real screen
Make the Capital One CodeSignal mock what Pro buys, wrapped in the countdown
card. Effort M. **Blocked until 09-29**, and pointless while the door has no
handle — charging for something nobody finds measures nothing.

### Approach C: Build the outcome loop first
One question after a target date passes: what happened? First outcome data this
product would ever have. Effort S. Ruled out as *first* because it delays
revenue past the 10-09 checkpoint, not because it is wrong — it is item two.

### Approach D: All three on the calendar
Rejected as the opening move: three things in motion across two open reads.

## Recommended Approach

**A, then C, then B** — with A as the thing that ships next.

A is the binding constraint. Every other option is downstream of people being
able to reach the feature. It is activation rather than monetisation, so it
clears the 09-29 freeze; it is a flag flip plus routing against code that is
already written and tested; and it is what makes B measurable when B becomes
legal on 09-29.

Ship A after the 09-13 read closes, not before — it changes first-contact
routing, which is what that read measures.

## Open Questions

- **Does the third entry point at `src/app.jsx:30842` still render?** It is a
  button inside what appears to be an empty state. If it is dead, there are two
  doors, not three.
- **Should `job_ready` route the same as `interview`?** They behave differently
  at the modal (3.5% vs 7.5%) but both are hiring intent. Routing them together
  doubles the population and blurs the read; routing only `interview` halves it.
  Recommend routing both and splitting them in the read.
- **Which mock does a routed user land on?** Seven of eight are generic. A user
  routed to "Tech Startup" learns nothing about their own screen. Landing them
  on the eight-item list may be worse than landing them on nothing.

## Success Criteria

`interview_prep_funnel` (`docs/agent/metrics.md`), distinct people by
`COALESCE(aid, username)`: `reached_card` → `set_a_target` → `saw_a_plan` →
`opened_an_item`.

**Baseline is structurally zero** — the events do not exist before the flip.
The honest first read is reach, not conversion: does the population that touches
the interview surface move from **22 lifetime** to something that can carry a
rate at all. Under 60 in 30 days means routing did not solve discovery and the
door is somewhere else.

**Guardrail:** first-contact activation must not fall. Routing declared-interview
users away from the beginner ladder sends them somewhere harder, and the 105
opener exists because the first seat matters.

## Dependencies

- 2026-09-13 activation read must close first.
- `intentRouting` and `interviewCountdown` are separate flags. A needs only the
  first; B needs the second and the 09-20 read.
- Mock events must carry a mock id before any of this is readable. Today they
  carry none — that instrumentation is a prerequisite for A's read, not a
  follow-up.

## The Assignment

**Write to the 22 accounts that have interview history and ask what happened.**

They found a feature with no navigation entry, which means they wanted it enough
to arrive through a deep link or an onboarding branch. They are the only people
who have ever used the hiring product, nobody has ever asked them anything, and
their answers are simultaneously the first outcome data this product would have,
the raw material for the open review-ask claim, and the only honest basis on
which a readiness number could ever be built.

One question, founder voice, per `CLAUDE.md`. Not a survey.
