## Recommended order
1. **Smoke bot + rollback** — protects everything below it; two outages this
   month were found by people.
2. **Annual: remove the false "most people choose this"** — a one-line fix to
   a claim on the buying surface. The rest of the annual work after the
   2026-10-03 modal read.
3. **Company mocks section + page structure** — correctness; cheap.
4. **Explain-approach in more mocks** — cheap, content only.
5. **Pattern SEO pages** — content we already own; Bing first.
6. **Landing comparison** — before 2026-10-04 (hero CTA test) or after its
   read, not across it.
7. **Post-hire track** with tickets as its first content — the biggest bet,
   and the one aimed at churn after the interview.
8. Query review, refactor drills, dirty dataset — as the track proves out.
9. Dialect notes; the Postgres toggle only if the notes show demand.
- Stripe / Databricks mocks whenever a dated source exists — not before.

## New metrics these plans propose
`post_hire_retention`, `ticket_completion`, `query_review_use`,
`refactor_pass_rate`, `target_db_mix`, `approach_box_use`. None is in
`docs/agent/metrics.md` yet. Each gets its definition and a measured baseline
at the start of its build, not now — a baseline taken months before the work
is the stale-number trap this repo has hit before.
