# Funding and credits: where we apply, in what order (2026-11-25)

Founder's ask, 2026-11-25: "Nerelere başvuralım bi plan hazırla ve uygulamaya başla."
Research: web search on 2026-11-25. Sources at the bottom. Every date is
re-checked on the program's own page before submitting.

## Where we stand (why the order is what it is)

- Traction is small: **3 active subscribers, 2 payers in 30 days**
  (`docs/agent/objectives.md`, `docs/agent/scorecard.md`; re-read before
  quoting). O2 targets 10 payers / 30 days by 2027-02-08.
- One founder. Legal entity Datrick, Inc. (DBA SQL Quest). No VC.
- The AI tutor runs on Anthropic. Hosting is Vercel + Supabase.

So: programs that need no traction go first (credits). Equity programs come
after the numbers move, timed to O2's checkpoints. EU grants are out: they
want deep tech or an R&D consortium, and a company established in the EU or
Türkiye.

   from the draft and submit. Same for AWS Activate.
2. **This week:** fill the `[LAPTOP]` numbers in the YC and Emerge drafts from

## Rules

- Every number in an application comes from a dated read. No estimate is
  written as a measurement. "300+" for the bank, never the exact total.
- No claim about how often anything is asked in interviews, and nothing about
  Capital One's process stated as Capital One's own.
- No customer name in any application or in this repo.
- Sending, submitting and signing are the founder's. Agents draft.
