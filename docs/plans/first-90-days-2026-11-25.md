## 1. The finding in one paragraph

The track was built and nobody is in it. Since 2026-10-04 one real person
has set the `hired` status, and nobody outside the team has opened a ticket.
The door to the track is the outcome question, and the email that asks it has
never been sent. So the problem is not the content. It is that the product
never learns who got the job. The people who do reach "first job" shape are a
different, larger group: job-ready learners and Analyst Day-One goal holders.
They never see a ticket, because tickets sit behind the `hired` status.

## 3. The problem in their words

**We do not have their words, and this plan says so.** In 15 feedback
messages, none is about a job or work. Of 3,389 tutor questions from 657
people, none is about a stakeholder, a manager or the job itself. The inbox
holds one job question from a payer, still unanswered
(`docs/reads/inbox-review-2026-11-23.md`). Every reason in this plan comes
from behaviour, not from what people said. That is why step 1 asks.

## 4. What to build, ranked (smallest useful first)

There is no new engine. Each step reuses `post-hire.js`, `tickets.js`,
`ticket-grade.js`, `setUserIntent`, and the Coach card.

### F1 · Ask the outcome inside the app, and count who sees the door — S

- **What.** One line on the Coach and on the Interview tab: "Did your
  interview happen?", with three answers: passed / not this time / not yet.
  - **Who sees it:** people with intent `interview` or `job_ready`.
  - **When:** either their `prepTarget.date` has passed, or they come back
    21+ days after first stating the intent.
  - **How often:** once per person per 30 days. Skippable.
- **What the answers do.**
  - The answer writes the existing `interview_outcome`, with
    `source='in_app'`. "Not yet" is stored as `moved`, which keeps the enum.
  - "Passed" opens the existing offer ask (`shouldAskOffer`), which leads to
    `hired`.
- **New exposure events.** `outcome_ask_shown {surface, trigger}` and
  `first90_link_shown`, so the door has a denominator.
- **Why first.** Without people in the status, nothing else in the track can
  be read. The email door was never opened, so the in-app ask is the cheapest
  door. It does not depend on an outward send.
- **Expected size.** About 14 eligible people a month. This is a
  measurement step, not a growth step.

### F2 · Ticket 1 for the people who already look like "first job" — S

- **What.** A Coach card, "A request from a PM, on real data", opens ticket 1.
  - **Who sees it:** Analyst Day-One holders and `job_ready` people with 6+
    solves, not only `hired`.
  - **What stays the same:** `FREE_TICKETS = 1` stays, and tickets 2–5 stay
    Pro, through the existing `post_hire` modal reason.
  - **New event:** `ticket_card_shown {source}`, where `source` is
    `analyst_day_one`, `job_ready` or `hired`.
- **Why.** This group is about 30 times bigger than the `hired` group (226
  job-ready solvers in 90 days against 1 hired person ever). It is also the
  goal people move through best: Analyst Day-One's 23% step rate is the
  highest of the three goals.
- **Why it fits O2.** The ticket's shape is to read a stakeholder's question,
  choose the grain, and answer with one figure. Our dossiers document that
  shape inside interview loops:
  - Wise's own analyst take-home (`docs/reads/companies/wise.md`);
  - Ramp's take-home case (`ramp.md`);
  - Meta's "define the metric first" (`meta.md`);
  - Shopify's "explain the results" rounds (`shopify.md`).

  So a ticket is also case-round practice for the buyer we already have. Say
  it that way only where a dossier backs it.

### F3 · Seven more tickets, only if F2 is used — M (content only)

- **Gate.** Only after F2's read: at least 10 people solve ticket 1.
- **What.** Seven tickets on the datasets of the signed company sets:
  - `finans_neobank` (Revolut);
  - `finans_fraud` (Capital One);
  - one e-commerce dataset.
- **Rules.**
  - Each ticket has a reference query and figures that
    `tests/post-hire.test.js` recomputes.
  - A ticket with a trap gets a wrong answer that must fail.
  - No company is named in a ticket (the `tickets.js` rule).
- **Why gated.** Five tickets with zero opens do not need twelve.

### F4 · Keep Pro useful for a payer who passed — S (copy) + founder

- **What.** When a Pro payer answers "passed", the Coach opens the First 90
  Days card, and one line says what Pro still opens: the tickets and the
  bank. There is no price change and no discount.
- **Precondition (founder).** Cancellation reasons in the Stripe portal
  (M-05) and PR #242 deployed. Without a reason on the cancel,
  `post_hire_retention` cannot tell "interview over" from "price".

## 6. How it connects to revenue

- **Retention after the interview.** Today no monthly payer has a kept second
  month. If even one payer a month stays two more months because the product
  still has a job for them, that is $58, the same as two extra
  first-month sales at $29.
- **Pro value at the wall.** Through F2, tickets 2–5 become a Pro reason for
  job-ready people, the group that clicks checkout at 3.5%
  (`docs/designs/hiring-readiness-module.md`). The existing `post_hire` modal
  reason counts it.
- **Annual.** "You will need it after the interview too" is the honest case
  for $99 a year over $29 a month. Use it only after F4 shows that hired
  payers stay.
- **B2B.** Not now (0 `team_interest` rows).
- **Ceiling, stated plainly.** F1 + F4 touch about 1–3 people a month. This
  track protects payers. It does not create them. F2 is the only step with an
  O2-sized audience.

## 8. Kill criteria (written before the data)

- **F1.**
  - **Read:** 28 days after the flip. If it merges by 2026-12-04, the read
    is on 2027-01-01.
  - **Success:** answered ÷ shown ≥ 30% at ≥ 15 people shown.
  - **Kill:** below 15% at ≥ 15 shown → remove the ask; people do not want
    to be asked.
  - **Unreadable:** fewer than 15 shown by the read → extend once by 28
    days, then remove.
- **F2.**
  - **Read:** 28 days after the flip (2027-01-01 at the latest).
  - **Success:** opened ÷ shown ≥ 10% at ≥ 40 shown, and ≥ 40% of openers
    solve.
  - **Kill:** fewer than 5% open at ≥ 40 shown → remove the card, and
    tickets go back behind `hired`.
  - **Guardrail:** `reach_6_rate` of card-shown people may not fall more
    than 5 points below the same goal's 28 days before the flip. If it does,
    the card is pulling people off challenges → remove it.
- **F3.** Not built unless F2 reads ≥ 10 ticket-1 solvers. Then read: locked
  ticket opens → `pro_plan_clicked` within 30 minutes. If 0 clicks from
  ≥ 15 locked opens, tickets 2+ do not sell Pro → stop adding tickets.
- **F4.**
  - **Read:** 60 days after the first hired payer (the existing metric's
    window).
  - **Falsification:** hired payers cancel at the same rate as payers whose
    date passed without the status.
  - n will be 1–3; read the verbatims as well.
