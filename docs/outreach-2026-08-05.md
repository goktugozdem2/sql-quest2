## Segment 1b — Reached checkout, and our button failed them (2)

Found 2026-08-05 while looking for more people like pupsiiik. These two are
not hesitation, they are a defect:

```
yisus      03:54:31 lifetime → 03:54:51 annual → 03:55:04 monthly
           → 03:55:11 dismissed        all three plans in 33 seconds
hrishi998  18:37:16 monthly  → 18:37:36 monthly AGAIN → 18:37:53 annual
```

Nobody comparison-shops by clicking checkout three times in half a minute, and
nobody clicks the same plan twice twenty seconds apart. yisus fired three
checkout clicks with no `modal_shown` between them and a `modal_dismissed` at
the end — one page session throughout, so the browser never navigated.

The mechanism was in the build they ran. On 2026-07-15 the handler recorded
`pro_checkout_clicked`, then hit an unguarded `JSON.parse` of localStorage,
then set `window.location.href`. If that parse throws, the handler dies after
the analytics call and before the navigation — so our funnel records a click
that could never convert, and their screen does nothing. Commit 90e315f
wrapped that read in try/catch on 07-17 as a side effect of the receipt-email
refactor. Fixed by accident, never noticed.

**Both drafts are apologies, not pitches.** They claim what is observable (the
page did not open, it was our fault, it is fixed) rather than the exact
mechanism, which is strongly indicated but not provable per-user.

## How to read the replies

Do not average them. At this n the value is in the specific sentence, not the
distribution — the same reason §18 refuses to add an NPS score. Look for the
one that names a challenge, a screen, or a moment. That is a fix.

Log anything durable into `scripts/funnel-report.sql` §16 (what users actually
told us) so the next read inherits it.
