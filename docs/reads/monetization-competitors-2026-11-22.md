## What the plan takes from this (pattern, not page copy)

- **Nobody in the set runs a card-required free trial.** The two vendors read
  today sell pay-now, one with a money-back guarantee (StrataScratch, 5 days),
  one with a $5/month-equivalent annual (DataLemur). Our 7-day card-required
  trial is the outlier; our modal also states a 7-day money-back guarantee.
- **Both read today sell a one-time option** (DataLemur's $300 bundle,
  StrataScratch's $289 lifetime with a standing 30% code). We sell none since
  the $199 lifetime was removed on 2026-09-12 (zero purchases ever).
- **Our list prices sit above both** on monthly ($29 vs $15 / $24) and between
  them on annual ($99 vs $60 / $139).

None of these points is a reason by itself to change a price; the plan uses
them only as context next to our own funnel numbers.
