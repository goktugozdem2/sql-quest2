alanlara ne kadar talep var, ek başka hangi ürünleri sunabiliriz, bizim kitle

Two reads, run apart on purpose (README 7b: the web read saw no customer

- **Market:** `docs/reads/market-demand-2026-11-22.md`. Web only, 29 dated

## 1. How much demand is there?

**In hiring, SQL is still the skill named most often.** These counts come
from job postings. They are mentions in postings, not how often a skill is
tested in an interview.

| Skill | Data-analyst postings | Note |
|---|---|---|
| SQL | 53–79% | first in every study read |
| Excel | 35–50% | |
| Python | 31–53% | 57% in data-scientist postings |
| Power BI | 29–40% | 79% in BI-titled roles (small sample) |
| Tableau | 26–40% | |
| Statistics | 12–38% | |
| R | ~25% | |
| A/B testing | 7% | |

- **Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2025:** SQL 58.6%, Python 57.9%, with
  Python up 7 points on 2024.
- **BLS projections, 2025–35:** data scientists +35%, operations research
  analysts +12%.

**Search demand we receive is SQL only, because we only cover SQL.**

- **Google, 90 days:** 581 clicks on 36,288 impressions, 63% of the clicks
  on the brand.
- **Adjacent topics on Google:** Python 3 impressions, pandas 1. Excel, Power
  BI, Tableau, statistics and A/B testing had 0, all time.
- **Bing, 90 days:** 782 clicks, 75% on "sql practice / exercises / questions".
- **Read zero as "we have no page", not "nobody searches".** Both consoles
  only report queries we already appear for.

**What our people tell us is SQL for an interview.**

- Of 961 people with a stated goal, 348 named an interview, 216 job-ready,
  213 learning and 183 exploring.
- 146 people named a target company; 64% of those companies are fintech (top
  four: Wise, Revolut, Snowflake, Capital One). Part of this is where people
  arrived, since a company-page link sets the company.
- Only **6** people have an interview date on record.
- 0 `interview_outcome` events, ever.
- **No one asked for another tool.** That covers 7 real feedback messages and
  the tutor text.

**The adjacent demand inside our audience is still SQL, in another setting:**

- dialects (Snowflake is the third most-named target, and its search demand
  is "snowflake sql practice");
- dbt, BigQuery and Spark CTEs;
- "data science SQL" interviews.

## 2. What our audience pays for

**Our payers, from Stripe webhook rows only:**

- 9 people all time, 6 in the last 90 days.
- 6 monthly and 3 annual; 3 paid after a trial, 6 directly.
- Median 6 solves before paying (n = 9).
- Payers arrived from company pages (3), the homepage (2), practice pages
  (`/sql-exercises/`, `/sql-tutorial/`) and an AI assistant referral.
- None had an interview date on record.
- After paying they keep solving SQL, and 4 started a mock interview.

**The ask that works** (modal → checkout click rate):

| Modal | Clicked checkout |
|---|---|
| Free-quota wall | 13.0% (19 of 146) |
| Milestone modal | 1.8% (8 of 443) |
| Every other modal reason | 2 or fewer people each |

**India is where checkout clicks don't become payments.** India is 16 of the
46 people who clicked checkout (35%), and 1 of the 9 payers.

**What the market charges** (details and sources in the market read):

| Offer | Price |
|---|---|
| Practice subscriptions (DataLemur, StrataScratch, Analyst Builder, Let's Data Science, Maven) | $15–34/mo or $60–399/yr |
| Lifetime / coaching tiers | DataLemur $300 one-time with coaching; StrataScratch $289 lifetime |
| Human mock interview, data-analyst coaches (Prepfully) | $99–279 a session |
| Excel / financial-modelling prep (Wall Street Prep, BIWS) | $39 course, $497–1,497 packages, $800/h tutoring |
| Data-analytics bootcamps | $7k–18k |
| Google Data Analytics certificate (Coursera) | $49/mo, under $300 for most |

SQL Quest has nothing above $99 a year.

## 3. What we can offer (ranked; the founder decides)

1. **Sell more of SQL interview prep, higher up the price ladder.**
   - A premium tier: one founder-led mock interview, or a review of a
     sitting.
   - The market pays $99–279 a session, and it needs no build.
   - **Price and money flags are the founder's decision**, so this is a
     proposal. Test it by hand first: one sentence in E1's drafts to trial
     users, then count the replies.
2. **SQL dialects:** Snowflake, BigQuery and PostgreSQL. This is the clearest
   adjacent demand inside our own data (the target companies, the Snowflake
   queries), and the PostgreSQL-mode spike already exists (PR #175, a draft).
3. **Python/pandas as a module, behind a demand test.** The market ranks it
   next, and others sell it at $15–34/mo. Our audience has not asked for it,
   so test it before building: 10 questions inside SQL Quest plus a
   waitlist. The proposed kill rule: fewer than 50 sign-ups in 30 days. The
   founder sets the final number in the ledger first.
4. **Graded PL-300 / DAX practice.** This is the clearest gap in the market:
   the practice we found is multiple-choice only, with nothing that grades a
   DAX measure. It is a different buyer and hard to build (DAX in the
   browser). Later, if at all.
5. **Teams (B2B):** `/for-teams/` is live in the repo from 11-22 and waits on
   its 30-day test. Maven charges $39 a seat; DataCamp Teams $11 a user.

**Not now:** Excel/financial-modelling tests. They have the highest
willingness to pay, but a different buyer (finance) with no overlap with our
traffic.

## 4. What this changes for O2 (10 payers per 30 days)

A new product does not move O2 by 2027-02-08. These do:

- **India checkout to payment** (16 clickers, 1 payer). This is B1's next
  funnel question: card or 3D Secure failures at checkout, and the Payment
  Link fallback.
- **The quota wall converts at 13%.** More people reaching it, at about 6
  solves, beats any new modal.
- **The interview date is almost never captured** (6 people). The
  date-driven asks (the deadline offer, the countdown) have almost no one to
  run on. This is a measurement gap; give it to B1/B2.
- **Option 1 above** (premium mock) is the only new offer that could add
  revenue inside the O2 window.
