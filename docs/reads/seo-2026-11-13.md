(weekly buckets to 11-06), `bing_crawl_daily` (to 11-05), `bing_url_status`

The memory note "Bing is the real channel" is no longer true. Google has out-clicked Bing every week since 10-12.

### Where the Bing loss sits
Almost all of it is one page, `/sql-exercises/`:
- Clicks went from 227 a week (bucket 10-02) to 46 (11-06).
- Impressions went from 3,686 to 1,119.
- Average position held at 6–7.

The query slice shows two separate causes.

**Ranking.** On Bing's head term "sql practice", the page slipped from position 8 to 10 in the bucket of 10-16 and has stayed there. Its impressions fell from ~700 a week to 70–500.

**Demand.** Every query lost impressions at roughly the same position. That includes the brand query "sql quest": 170 → 95 impressions at position 3–4.

| query (Bing, 2 weeks) | imp 09-25/10-02 | clicks | pos | imp 10-30/11-06 | clicks | pos |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| sql practice questions | 436 | 46 | 3.5 | 251 | 11 | 4.5 |
| sql quest | 170 | 43 | 3.0 | 95 | 21 | 4.0 |
| sql practice | 1,769 | 29 | 7.5 | 609 | 7 | 10.0 |
| sql practice exercises | 121 | 23 | 2.0 | 58 | 5 | 3.0 |

Nothing on `/sql-exercises/` changed after 09-30. Its last commits are the five worked solutions and the 67-character title. Bing recrawled it on 11-10. So no site change explains the slip.

Bing's index count rose from 173 to 774 (sitemap: 416), so Bing knows pages the sitemap does not list. `bing_url_status` covers only sitemap URLs, so the extra ~360 cannot be listed from the API. They are probably parameterised `/app/?…` URLs from before the 09-23 robots rule.

**Not concluded:** that the question pages, the free quota or anything else we shipped caused the slip. The data cannot separate a Bing ranking change from our own.

### Brand demand halved on both engines
Google "sql quest" + "sqlquest" to the homepage:
- Window A: 279 impressions, 133 clicks.
- Window B: 138 impressions, 42 clicks.

Bing's brand query fell by the same proportion. This is fewer people searching our name. It is not an SEO problem, and the fix is not on a page.

### Trap pages (plan §3, due 10-23)
**PASS on the letter.** The rule was "≥ 2 in the top 20 and ≥ 500 impressions → the next five patterns":
- All five pages sit at positions 5.3–7.0 on Google.
- Since 09-25 they have 680 Google impressions and 18 on Bing.

But the pages have **6 clicks in seven weeks**, and the visible queries include pasted exam questions. "you perform a left join … what is the most likely reason? a. … b. …" is someone, or an assistant, searching an assignment.

Before writing five more pages, the founder should weigh that against the work. Every number on a trap page needs its own query on `finans_fraud`. The rule does not ask about clicks; a reasonable person would.

### Question pages (plan §3, due 10-27)
**TRIGGERED.** The rule was "if CTR stays under 1.5% at position ≤ 10, the lever is the generator's title/description template". Window B shows 4,490 impressions at position 7.6 with 29 clicks, which is **0.65%**.

One caveat is written now:
- 588 of those impressions are one page, `/questions/recursive-team-size-rollup/`, for "rollup interview questions". That searcher means `GROUP BY ROLLUP`, which SQLite does not have.
- Without that page, CTR is 0.74%. That is still under the bar.
- Most of the question pages' queries are anonymised, so intent can't be read further.

**→ Shipped 2026-11-13: the template change (ledger: "the question pages' snippet fits the window").**
