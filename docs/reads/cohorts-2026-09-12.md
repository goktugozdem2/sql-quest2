## What it says

- **Registered accounts activate.** Half of every cohort reaches six solves
  (36–67%, no trend), and seven in ten solve at least one. Activation is not
  where registered users are lost; it is where guests are lost, before they
  ever sign up (docs/agent/objectives.md, 663 → 314).
- **They do not pay.** Three payers in 268 signups since 06-29: 1.1%, and
  never two in one week. Every cohort with a payer had one, in the week the
  person arrived, at 6–10 solves — the first-session pattern again
  (docs/reads/purchase-timing-2026-09-09.md). No cohort has produced a payer
  later than its first days, which is the fact the second ask
  (`activated-note`, batch 1 sent 2026-09-12 20:57Z, 40 people) is testing.
- **The population is pre-selected.** Most accounts are created after a
  first solve (the post-solve prompt), so "signup" here already means "solved
  and chose to keep it"; the guest → signup step is the larger funnel and is
  read by `aid` in the Friday table, not here.

## Traps

- `users.created_at` was overwritten by every client save until 2026-09-12;
  read `data.createdAt`. The wrong column moved both monthly payers into
  the outage week.
- Cohorts younger than 14 days are still maturing on both columns; the
  09-07 row is also the outage, so it reads nothing.
- The 09-21 `freeQuota` flip changes what a cohort can do after ten solves;
  read cohorts from that week on against the ones before it, not as one line.
