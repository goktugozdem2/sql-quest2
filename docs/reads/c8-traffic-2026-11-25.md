## Method

- **Windows.**
  - Y = 2026-11-24 00:00 → 24:00 UTC.
  - Baseline = 11-17 → 11-23, given as a daily average (7-day sum ÷ 7).
  - People are counted per day: someone active on two days counts twice in
    the sum, as a daily average should.
- **Human filter.** The A01 filter from PR #387 (`scripts/scorecard-weekly.sql`,
  not yet on main), applied as written.
  - That includes `utc1280` and `cn_app`.
  - It also includes the excluded aid `363dfb2b…` and the founder's 11-20
    tests and 11-24 walk guests.
  - Also excluded:
    - the A2 hermetic-walk aids (`a2a2a2a2…0`, `…1`, `66017b70…`). None of
      them appears in `pro_events`, so the walk stubs held.
    - **new here, `google_ua`:** a `landing_view.ua` matching
      `GoogleOther|Google-NotebookLM`, with no `app_opened`.
- **Source.** The first non-empty `landingSrc` of the person's day.
  - Brave and Yahoo arrive as `ref:search.brave.com` and `ref:*.yahoo.com`,
    and are grouped as search.
  - `ref:buy.stripe.com`, `ref:checkout.stripe.com` and `utm:blog-*` are
    returns to our own pages ("internal return").
- **Money.** `stripe_webhook` rows only, joined by username.
