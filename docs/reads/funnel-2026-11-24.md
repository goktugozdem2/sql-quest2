says the guest path shows it to ~98% of first solvers within 90 s. #282
holds the streak card in arm `one`, so the arm covers it, but its walk
undercounts the control by one layer.

**Do more layers mean fewer second solves?** `second_solve_same_session`

| Band | First solvers | 2nd solve same session |
|---|---|---|
| desktop | 205 | 134 (65%) |
| mobile | 6 | 4 (67%) |
| tablet | 3 | 2 |

By the layers that leave a row (desktop; mobile n = 6 is not split):

| Group | n | 2nd solve same session |
|---|---|---|
| answered the intent modal | 160 | 112 (70%) |
| intent from a link, no modal | 34 | 20 (59%) |
| no intent at all (met the modal, never answered) | 11 | 2 (18%) |

- **No dose-response on desktop.** The 2-layer group does best; the 0–1

  not answer. Only the 3+ group (n = 8) points the way #282 expects.
- **Mobile cannot be read**: 6 first solvers in 28 days, all of them met
  the streak card and answered the intent modal; 4 solved again. That is
  #282's own small-sample rule (under 15 per arm is directional).
- What this means for #282: the overload hypothesis is not supported or
  refuted by desktop data, and mobile is too thin to say. The test stands on
  the walk (W3) and on its own arms, not on a pre-existing correlation.
  Four of the layers it changes emit nothing, so the test can be read only
  on the outcome metric.

### 8e. Queued design — "Next question" first, after solves 1–5 (desktop)

**Queued, not built.** It starts only when all three hold:
1. a fleet slot is free (8d);
2. the 11-22 read's pre-registered 1 → 6 rule (read ~2026-12-13) says the
   handoff did not recover: the 11-23 → 12-06 cohorts' 1 → 6 **< 30%** with
   #94 → #95 **< 75%** → build it; **≥ 36%** → drop it (the `weakSkillNext`
   revert recovered the rate); 30–36% → re-read 12-22 with one more week of
   cohorts;
3. a 1366×768 walk confirms the premise below. If the Next quest card is
   already above the fold after a solve, the hypothesis is wrong and the
   design is dropped.

- **Hypothesis.** After a correct submit the "⚡ Next quest" card (app.jsx,
  `challengeStatus === 'success' && nextChallengeRec`) renders after the
  verdict, diagnosis, Your Output, Expected Output and the AI nudge, and
  its button ("Start →") has no keyboard path. People at 1–5 solves who
  carry on mostly do it some other way: in the 28 days before the 11-22
  revert the card was taken after 22% of solves 1–2 and 31% of solves 3–5,
  while a next solve came within 30 minutes after 73% / 66% of them, and
  16% / 12% left with no event in the following day. Putting the card
  directly under the verdict line, focused so Enter opens it, makes the
  next curriculum step the default action, and more solvers reach 3 and 6.
- **Arm.** Flag `nextUpFirst`, test `next_up_first_v1`. Assigned by aid
  with FNV + murmur3 finalizer (as `companyAskArm`), once, at the person's
  first correct submit while not Pro, with 1–5 distinct solves, on a
  non-`mobile:` viewport; event `next_up_first_assigned {testId, arm,
  solves}`. Must be independent of `company_direct_v1`, `run_submit_nudge_v1`
  and the two mobile tests (tested over 10,000 aids, 50 ± 3% agreement).
  `top`: the same card, same pick (`pickNextChallenge`, curriculum order),
