## Rules applied (written before this read)

- `docs/plans/seo-measurement-2026-09-13.md` §4, 28-day window, on pages at
  least 8 weeks past their first Google impression (every pruned or merged page
  is: first impressions 2026-05-25 → 2026-09-15):
  - ≥ 10 impressions, 0 clicks, position ≤ 20 → **IMPROVE**;
  - < 10 impressions and no landed people → **noindex**;
  - two pages on the same top query, the weaker < ⅓ of the stronger's clicks →
    **MERGE**;
  - the plan named `/microsoft-sql-interview/` and `/bloomberg-sql-interview/`
    "first in line" when they shipped.
- The founder's prune bar for this run, all four at once: near-zero
  impressions over 90 days AND no conversions (no first solve or checkout from
  its landings) AND no backlinks AND not a nav/legal page. Never pruned: `/`,
  `/app/`, legal, `/sql-exercises/`, the interview hub, converting company
  pages, frozen pages.
- **Landed people** here is the plan's "landed people" after the fleet's bot
  filters (README rule 2: `metadata.bot`, the five crawler viewports including
  `desktop:1024x1024`, `aocr.org`, UTC landers who never open the app,
  GoogleOther / NotebookLM and other bot user agents, internal usernames).
  The filter still leaves crawler traffic in: most pages show landed people
  and zero app opens. So "no landed people" is read as "no landed person
  opened the app" for the five prunes, and the table shows both numbers.
  Bing impressions were checked as well; none of the five has any.
- An **IMPROVE** row also covers ≥ 100 impressions in 28 days at CTR < 1%,
