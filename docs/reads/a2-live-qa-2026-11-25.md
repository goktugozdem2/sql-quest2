## Problem found, fixed

**The inline tutor's input row scrolls the page sideways on a phone.**
- **Where:** opening the tutor on a challenge at 375 or 320, from Help or from #234's new tutor link.
- **What happens:** the input, "Show me the answer" (socraticLadder) and Send share one flex row, and the input keeps its 196 px intrinsic width. The row runs to 456 px, so Send is off screen and `documentElement.scrollWidth` is 456 at 375.
- **Reproduced:** twice at each width.
- **Is it new?** No. The row predates today, but #234 now sends people to it from the 💬 widget.
- **Fix:** PR #379 wraps the row and gives the input a 12rem floor. On the local build the document is 375 at 375, and 1366 / 768 stay on one line. The test was proven by two breaks.
