# Funnel read — why six-solvers fell ~3.5× (B1, 2026-11-22)

Pulled forward from Tuesday by B2's O2 finding: six-solvers / 30 d went
223 (to 10-02) → 72 (to 11-10) → 64 (to 11-22) while six-solver → payer held
at 2.8–4.7%. Question: which factor explains how much of the drop.

Source: production `pro_events` through the read-only MCP, 2026-11-22.
People = `coalesce(aid, username)`. Out: `metadata.bot`, viewports
`tablet:800x600` / `desktop:1280x1200` / `desktop:1919x992`, `ref:aocr.org`,
the two pre-stamp fingerprints from `src/utils/bot-signals.js`
(`desktop:1920x1080` + `America/Los_Angeles` with no landingSrc,
`desktop:1280x720` + `UTC`), `wd` / `uaBot` true, and the internal usernames.
Search clicks: `bing_site_daily`, `gsc_daily` page slice (never summed
across slices), `bing_stats` page and query slices (weekly buckets).

Reproduction of B2's numbers on these filters: six-solvers in window
**223 / 529** solvers (to 10-02; B2 223 / 524), **75** (to 11-10; B2 72),
**63** (to 11-22; B2 64). The decomposition below uses windows
**A = 09-02 → 10-02** and **B = 10-23 → 11-22**.

## 1. Measurement — ruled out first

- **#49 (10-08) does not change how real people's solves or opens are
  counted.** It drops events only from a browser that declares itself
  automated (`navigator.webdriver`, or a bot user agent — the landing
  tracker's July rule) and only *stamps* the soft fingerprints (rows still
  written). `app_opened` still fires once per browser per UTC day after the
  session settles (`sqlquest_app_opened_day`); that code has not changed
  since September. `challenge_solved` fires on a first solve of an id,
  unchanged since September.
- **The `app_opened`-per-person halving is two things, neither a counting
  break.** (a) The 08-17 → 09-16 cohort has three months to accumulate days,
  the 11-01 → 11-14 cohort three weeks — the 2.13 vs 1.13 comparison is not
  like for like. (b) Returns really fell, and the test is that **any event
  on a later day** (not just `app_opened`) tracks the `app_opened` return
  within 0–2.5 points in every weekly cohort (e.g. 09-21: 23.0% vs 24.2%;
  11-02: 4.4% vs 6.8%). If `app_opened` had stopped firing on a return
  visit, the two would split. They do not.
- The 09-28 and 10-05 arrival weeks still hold an unfingerprinted crawler
  (379 and 685 "new" arrivals, ~5–6% first-run rate); arrival counts for
  those two weeks are not comparable. Solves are not affected (crawlers do
  not solve).

## 3. The decomposition

New first-solvers in the window, reach to six within 14 days of the first
solve (the cohort form of B2's number): **A 209 of 500 (41.8%) → B 57 of
222 (25.7%)**, a drop of 152.

six = first-solvers × (1 → 6 rate). In logs: ln(209/57) = 1.30 =
ln(500/222) **0.81 (62%)** + ln(41.8/25.7) **0.49 (38%)**.

| Factor | Share of the drop | Six-solvers lost (≈) |
|---|---|---|
| **Fewer people solve anything (volume)** | **~62%** | **~95** |
| – of which the `/sql-exercises/` door (Bing) | **~50% of the total** | ~77 |
| – of which the homepage door | ~14% of the total | ~21 |
| – other doors, net (company and best-sites −, topic pages +) | ~0–3% | ~0–5 |
| **Fewer first-solvers reach six (rate)** | **~38%** | **~57** |
| – door mix (more topic / company landers, who convert lower) | ~10% | ~15 |
| – within every door | ~28% | ~42 |
| Measurement (#49 or anything else) | 0 | 0 |

The door rows are a door-by-door log split (volume at A's rate, then the
rate); they sum to the aggregate within ~5 people.

|---|---|---|---|

`/sql-exercises/` alone accounts for **97 of the 152** (64%): 77 from
volume, 20 from rate.

### 3a. Arrivals — the volume half is Bing

- **Bing:** site clicks 292/wk (09-21) → 21/wk (11-16). Bing's
  `/sql-exercises/` page slice, the 4 weeks to 10-02 vs to 11-21: clicks
  **674 → 114**, impressions **11,296 → 3,290**, average position 6.0 → 7.3.
  It is an impression collapse, not a CTR problem. The same fall shows on
  the brand query (`sql quest` 360 → 177 impressions, 81 → 29 clicks) and on
  every practice query (`sql practice questions` 821 → 390 impr, pos 5.0 →
  6.3; `sql practice exercises` 279 → 132, pos 2.3 → 4.0; `sql practice`
  2,545 → 609, pos 8 → 10; `sql exercises` 209 → 94, pos 3.0 → 6.5) — a
  site-level fall, not one query.
- In the same weeks Bing's `in_index` went **195 (09-21) → 480 (09-28) →
  830 (11-16)** — the `/questions/` pages entering the index — while clicks
  peaked and fell. Timing, not proof; #72 (11-20) already noindexes the
  question pages for bingbot, and its read is 2027-01-08.
- **Google** is flat (125–211/wk; 4-week sums ~620–640 both windows).
  Google-door first solvers fell 141 → 107 and their 1 → 6 rate 39% → 22%:
  Google's loss is rate, not traffic.
- **Homepage:** `landing_view` on `/` 157–197/wk (mid-September) → 71–95/wk,
  and home → app fell from 41–51% to ~20% from the week of 10-05 — the hero
  CTA test arms 10-04 and sends three of its five arms to `/sql-quiz/` or the
  readiness test, by design. Its per-arm read (B2, 11-22): control 13.3%
  solved-one, "Test My SQL Skills" 8.7% — already a MISS for that arm. The
  test costs the six-solver count less than one person a week; the homepage
  loss is mostly fewer homepage visits.

### 3b. Activation — the rate half

- **The free quota (10 solves, 09-26) does not stop people before six.**
  `pro_modal_shown` below six solves: 9 people in window A, ~9 in B (all
  `company_set` in B); the only big modal is the six-solve milestone, at six.
  The 1 → 6 cohort rate held at **38–40% for the 09-28, 10-05 and 10-12
  cohorts**, all under the quota and the 09-30 P1 flags. Share stopping at
  exactly 10: 7% → 10%.
- **The 3-solve account ask (#98) is from 11-22 only** and is not in any
  number here. It and the `weakSkillNext` revert (#90) landed the same day:
  the 11-23+ cohorts read both at once.
- **Per-challenge solve-through on the ladder held** (openers with < 7
  solves, 09-02 → 10-11 vs 10-16 → 11-22): #92 88% → 83%, #93 89% → 87%,
  #94 79% → 75%, #95 86% → 95%, #106 56% → 57%, #107 61% → 67%. People who
  open a step solve it as before. The loss is **between a solve and the next
  step**: #94 solved → #95 opened 86% → 67%; first solvers stopping at
  exactly 4 went 9% → 14%.
- **The post-solve "next quest" lost its takers when `weakSkillNext`
  flipped (09-30):** among solves by people at 1–5 solves, a `next_rec_started`
  within 15 minutes fell 51–58% (09-14, 09-21) → 26–34% (10-05 onward), while
  "opened any challenge within 15 minutes" held at 86–92%. People kept going
  but off the ladder. Its own ledger claim is a MISS and it was reverted
  today (#90). It cannot be the whole story: the 10-05 cohort was fully under
  it and still reached six at 40%.
- **The trough is the 10-19 and 10-26 cohorts (19%, 18%)**, and no app
  change shipped between 10-16 (#57: registration path, tutor rate-limit
  RPC) and 11-20. Partial recovery to 28–33% since 11-02. This part is
  **not explained by a deploy**; traffic quality inside the doors (fewer
  brand and practice-query searchers from Bing, more deep links from topic
  and company pages) is the best remaining candidate, and the n is small
  (55–59 first solvers a week). Recorded as unexplained, not guessed.

### 3c. Returns (the `second_session_7d` question)

Return within 7 days fell in every group, not only among non-solvers (first
opens 08-17 → 09-16 vs 11-01 → 11-14): 0 solves 10.2% → 5.4%; 1–5 solves
24.5% → 7.0%; **6+ solves 56.3% → 26.9%**. The step is at the 09-28 cohort
(23.0% → 10.8%), the first week under the 10-solve quota. It moves
six-solvers little — reaching six on a return visit was 4–13 people a week
in September and is 2–3 now, because most six-solvers get there in their
first session (33–53 a week then, 7–13 now) — but it is the retention cost
of the quota and belongs to B4 / the founder's quota rule, not to activation.

## 4. Verdict and what B1 does

- **The largest factor is traffic: ~62% of the drop is fewer first solvers,
  and the `/sql-exercises/` Bing door alone is ~half of the whole drop.**
  Per the brief, B1 does not build for it: the finding goes to C1 as
  BACKLOG lines (pages and queries above).
- **The rate half has no single in-app cause B1 can isolate today.** The
  measured lever on it (`weakSkillNext`, the next-quest handoff) was reverted
  this morning, and #98 changes the step at three solves on the same day.
  Starting a third change on the same step now would make all three
  unreadable. **No experiment this run.** Next B1 run (Tuesday 11-24 and
  12-01): read the 11-23+ cohorts on 1 → 3, 3 → 6 and #94 → #95, split by
  `signupAskAtThree` exposure.
- Pre-registered for that read: if the 11-23 → 12-06 cohorts' 1 → 6 is
  **≥ 36%** (the October pre-trough level), the handoff revert recovered the
  rate and the remaining gap is traffic only. If it is **< 30%** with
  #94 → #95 still under 75%, B1's experiment is the next-step handoff after
  solve 3–5 (an A/B by aid on what the post-solve strip offers); if 3 → 6
  falls below the 11-09/11-16 level (≈ 44%) only among people shown the
  account ask, the ask goes back to the 10th solve.
