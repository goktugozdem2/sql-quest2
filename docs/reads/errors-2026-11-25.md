   #355 (draft, held: money path).**

     19:00 its first charge failed. Stripe reported `attempt_count` 0, no
     `next_payment_attempt` and status `past_due`.

     Within two seconds it wrote `pro_access_revoked {payment_failed_final,
     had_access: true}` and sent the "Stripe has stopped retrying, so Pro is
     paused" email.

     open invoice that waits for the customer. The next morning the person
     opened the app, met the Pro modal again (no trial offer) and closed it.

     gave_up revokes. A waiting charge gets an email that points to the
     invoice page.

     row in the window carries `after_trial`, and neither email row carries
     `kind` / `invoice_link`. So M-01 (11-22) never reached production:
     both emails went out with the old "reply and I'll send you a link"
     copy and no invoice button. BACKLOG A3-4.
