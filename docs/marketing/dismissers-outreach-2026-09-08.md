# Outreach: the people who closed the Pro screen

Drafted 2026-09-08. **Not sent.** Göktuğ sends these by hand, one at a time.

## Why this list exists

Measured 2026-09-08: over 60 days the Pro modal reached 282 people and produced
12 checkout clicks (4.3%); a third of clickers pay. The break is shown→click,
and we have never asked a single person who closed it what they were thinking.

The one decision-level sentence we own came from an email reply, not from the
in-app widget: payer #2, *"Have an interview coming up so I needed the
practice."* This list is an attempt to get eleven more sentences like that.

## The ask is ONE question, and it is not "why didn't you buy"

Open-ended on purpose. The plan
(`docs/plans/signup-to-subscriber-2026-09-08.md`) proposes replacing our intent
question with a branch that includes an **exam** option we do not currently
offer — and "is exam a real segment or a box I invented" is exactly the thing
to learn from people's own words *before* writing a button for it. Offering
categories in the email would contaminate that.

The dismissal is the **reason we are writing**, not a second question. Nobody is
asked to justify not paying.

## What to do with the answers

Every reply goes into the plan's question-design section as evidence for or
against the branch. Specifically:

- **If two or more say "exam" / "midterm" / "certification"** → the exam option
  is real, build it into Q1.
- **If the `learning`-intent people (razzgog, murugapalaniraja, manohar) turn
  out to have deadlines** → our `learning` bucket is mislabelled, and the 0
  clicks from it is a labelling artefact rather than a fact about them.
- **If nobody names a sector unprompted** → drop the sector branch; it is our
  interest, not theirs.
- **If they name a role (analyst / engineer / scientist)** → the role question
  earns its place; if nobody does, it does not.

Send no more than four a day, spread across the day. Thirteen inside a minute
is the fingerprint bulk filters look for (`docs/outreach-2026-08-05.md`).
